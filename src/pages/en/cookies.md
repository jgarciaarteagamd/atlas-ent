## A single cookie, to remember light or dark mode

This website **does not use analytics, advertising or third-party cookies**, or other tracking technologies such as pixels, local storage for analytics or browser fingerprinting.

The only cookie is a first-party technical one, and it is only set if you press the light/dark mode button in the header:

| Name | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `theme` | Remembers whether you chose light mode (`light`) or dark mode (`dark`), on this site, on the clinic page (jgarciaarteaga.pro) and on the professional background page (bio.jgarciaarteaga.pro). | 1 year |

It holds no personal data and cannot be used to identify you. As it is needed for a feature you request, it is exempt from consent under Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings; the site will then follow your device's mode again.

The site also does not load resources from external services (fonts, videos, maps or social networks) that could place cookies in your browser. That is why no cookie banner is shown.

## If this changes

If any cookie that is not strictly necessary were added in the future, such as an analytics tool, this policy would be updated and your consent would be requested before it is set, in line with Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI-CE.
