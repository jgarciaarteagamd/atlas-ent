## What data is processed and why

**1. If you write to the contact email.** Your email address, your name if you give it and the content of your message are processed only to reply to you.

- Legal basis: your consent when you write (Art. 6.1.a of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR).
- Retention: as long as needed to deal with your message, and afterwards for any legally required period.
- **Please do not send health information** (symptoms, reports, test results). This email is not a medical consultation channel, and questions about personal cases will not be answered.

**2. When you visit the site.** The hosting provider automatically logs technical data for each visit (IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type) to serve the site and protect it against attacks.

- Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
- These logs are not used to identify you or to build profiles.
